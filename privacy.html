Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Who we are

DEME Housepainting is a family-run interior and exterior house painting business. This page explains what information we collect through our website, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address and/or phone number
  • Any project details you choose to share with us

We do not collect any other personal information, and we do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies of any kind on this website.

How your enquiry is processed

When you submit the contact form, your details are sent through a secure automated email service (Resend) which formats your enquiry and delivers it directly to our business inbox. This happens automatically, in real time, the moment you submit the form. We do not maintain a separate database or store your submission anywhere other than in our own email inbox.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss and carry out painting work you have requested. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies. No tracking or analytics scripts are currently in place. If this changes in the future - for example, if we add website analytics - this policy will be updated accordingly, and a cookie consent notice will be added if required.

Third-party services

This site relies on the following third-party services to operate:

  • Vercel - website hosting
  • Resend - automated email delivery for the contact form

These providers may process data as necessary to deliver their service (for example, routing your enquiry email), governed by their own privacy policies.

Data retention

We retain enquiry emails for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and manage any ongoing or completed work. You can request that we delete your information at any time - see below.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, contact us using the details below.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at hello@demehousepainting.ie.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
