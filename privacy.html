Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

DEME Housepainting is a family-run interior and exterior house painting business operated by Benjamin Deme and Zoltan Deme. Together, they bring two generations of experience and care to every project, from single-room painting jobs to full exterior repaints.

Benjamin Deme and Zoltan Deme are responsible for handling personal information submitted through this website and responding to enquiries.

For privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact us at demehousepainting@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address and/or phone number
  • Any project details you choose to share with us

We do not collect any other personal information through this website. We do not use cookies, advertising, or cross-site tracking of any kind.

Website analytics

To understand how many people visit the site and which pages are useful, we use Vercel Web Analytics. It records anonymous, aggregated page-view information only, such as:

  • The page visited and the time of the visit
  • The referring website, if any
  • Approximate location (country and region)
  • Device type, operating system, and browser

Vercel Web Analytics is designed to provide aggregated website usage statistics without using cookies or storing visitors' IP addresses. It helps us understand general website traffic and usage patterns. Vercel may process technical information, such as device and browser details, to provide this service. For further information, please refer to Vercel's privacy policy.

How your enquiry is processed

When you submit the contact form, your details are securely transmitted to Resend, our third-party email delivery provider. Resend processes the information to format and deliver your enquiry to our business inbox. We do not operate a separate database for contact form submissions. Once delivered, your enquiry is retained in our business email inbox in accordance with our data retention practices. Resend may also process and retain information as necessary to provide its service, in accordance with its own privacy policy.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss and carry out painting work you have requested. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Legal basis for processing

We process your personal information where it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, such as preparing a quotation or discussing a painting project.

Where necessary, we may also process information to fulfil contractual or legal obligations, or to pursue legitimate business interests, such as responding to general enquiries and managing customer relationships.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies, including for analytics (see "Website analytics" above). Because nothing is stored on your device, no cookie consent notice is required. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly and a consent notice will be added if required.

Third-party services

This site relies on the following third-party services to operate:

  • Vercel - website hosting and anonymous website analytics
  • Resend - automated email delivery for the contact form

These providers may process data as necessary to deliver their service (for example, routing your enquiry email), governed by their own privacy policies.

Data retention

We retain enquiry emails only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, manage any resulting work, and meet relevant legal or business obligations. You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal requirements that may require us to retain certain records.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, contact us using the details below.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at demehousepainting@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy
